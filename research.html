Research

Football

Bayesian football forecasting

Do recent performances add useful information to team ratings? I study hierarchical models for football goal counts that combine Elo ratings with match-level covariates. I compare their predictions with simpler models using chronological evaluation and proper scoring rules.

With Roberto Macrì-Demartino, Leonardo Egidi, and Nicola Torelli.

Manuscript under review

Expected goals and season simulation

I model shot conversion probabilities from event data and use estimated scoring strengths to simulate matches and league tables. Recent work revisits Leicester City’s 2015/16 season through expected goals, examining how uncertainty in match outcomes affects the standings.

Leicester’s Tale — preprint
xG modelling — SIS 2025 proceedings

Cricket

Win probabilities during a run chase

I am developing ball-level models for runs, wickets, and delivery outcomes. Monte Carlo simulation of the remaining innings gives an estimate of the probability of a successful chase after each delivery.

Work in progress.
