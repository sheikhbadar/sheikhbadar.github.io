Research

Football

Bayesian football forecasting

Do recent performances add useful information to team ratings? I study hierarchical models for football goal counts that combine Elo ratings with match-level covariates. I compare their predictions with simpler models using chronological evaluation and proper scoring rules.

The CFE–CMStatistics abstract reports lower Brier and ranked probability scores than a standard attack–defence model in English Premier League evaluations. The five-league working paper asks whether recent performance improves forecasts beyond Elo.

Expected goals and season simulation

I model shot conversion probabilities from event data and use estimated scoring strengths to simulate matches and league tables. In our 2015/16 case study, Leicester ranked highly by midseason xG. Simulating the remaining fixtures showed how a title was possible even though it was unlikely.

Cricket

Win probabilities during a run chase

I am developing ball-level models for runs, wickets, and delivery outcomes. After each delivery, I use the updated runs needed, balls remaining and wickets in hand to simulate the rest of the innings and estimate the probability of a successful chase.

Work in progress.
